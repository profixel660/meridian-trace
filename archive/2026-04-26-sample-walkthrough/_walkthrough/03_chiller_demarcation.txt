1.1 FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

This Scope demarcation schedule is a point of reference for responsibilities, methods of delivery and works that are defined across the relevant
stakeholder and shall accompany all Technical Specifications. The intent of this document is to maintain consistency across the delivery of the
project and align all Trade Contractors and Supply Contracts.

1.2 CONTRACTED RESPONSIBILITY

The intent is for the Contractor to provide the works as documented on the Contract Documents. The documents are provided to industry
standard and the Contractor shall complete the design as would be reasonably expected on a project of this type. The responsibility matrix
below shall form part of the agreed. Provide due allowance in terms of design capability and programme to complete the works where required.

Provide due allowance in programme for this review process.
